# Commander Battle

**Commander Battle** is a multiplayer game mode for *Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord*, in the style of the commander battles of *Warband: Napoleonic Wars*.

Two armies meet on a campaign battlefield. Each side has 8 formations of AI soldiers, several hundred men in all, and a mounted general with his bodyguard. Players take command of a formation: they pick its troops, lead it from the front and give it orders. The team AI leads every formation nobody commands, so a battle is full with two players or with eighteen.

The mod comes with **RBM Multiplayer**, which brings the battle AI and the posture and stamina system of the Realistic Battle Mod (RBM) to Commander Battle.

## The battle in short

- **Attackers against defenders.** The last side with soldiers standing wins the round. If time runs out first, the defenders win.
- **Lead a formation.** Infantry, archers, cavalry or horse archers, in a light, medium or heavy weight, with the weapon setup you choose.
- **Or be the general.** Command the AI's formations with the order bar, suggest moves to the players, and draw orders on the map from high above the field.
- **Fall and fight on.** When your soldier dies, take over one of your own men and keep leading.
- **No flags, no gold, no morale.** Just the two armies.
