# Rounds & Rules

A Commander Battle match is a series of rounds between two armies, the **attackers** and the **defenders**. This page covers the armies, how a round is won, the battle rules and the maps.

## The armies

Each side is one culture's army: **8 formations** of AI soldiers, plus a **general** with a bodyguard of 6 mounted riders.

- **How many formations of each kind a side has depends on its culture.**

    | Culture | Infantry | Ranged | Cavalry | Horse archers |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | Vlandia | 3 | 2 | 3 | 0 |
    | Empire | 3 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
    | Khuzait | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
    | Aserai | 3 | 3 | 1 | 1 |
    | Sturgia | 5 | 1 | 2 | 0 |
    | Battania | 5 | 2 | 1 | 0 |

- **Each formation has a number,** the one the order bar uses. Every kind of formation has its usual numbers: infantry 1 and 6, ranged 2 and 5, cavalry 3, 7 and 8, horse archers 4. A culture with more formations of a kind than that gives them the lowest free numbers. Vlandia, for example, has infantry 1, 4 and 6, ranged 2 and 5, and cavalry 3, 7 and 8.
- **The general** is a 9th formation on top of the 8: a mounted hero in his culture's finest gear and his 6 elite riders. See [The General](general.md).
- **Army size depends on the troops.** Each formation's size is set by the weight of its troops: a heavy class fields fewer men, a light class more. Infantry and ranged formations are larger than cavalry and horse archer formations, because every rider also brings a horse. A Vlandian army with the AI's usual picks fields about 500 men. All heavy troops make it about 390, all light troops about 610. See [Commanding a Formation](commanding.md#troops-and-formation-size).

Players command the formations they take. **The team AI leads every formation nobody commands,** so a side always fights at full strength, however many players it has.

## A round

1. **Preparation.** The round opens with a short preparation time. Pick your formation and troops, and look over your side in the formation list (N). The formation sizes are worked out now, and again whenever someone changes troops.
2. **The armies spawn** in battle order and the fight begins. From now on, a change of troops waits for the next round.
3. **The round goes to the last side with soldiers standing.**
4. **If time runs out** with both armies still in the field, **the defenders win**. The attackers have to break them within the time limit.

Teams and formations carry over from round to round. After the last round of a match, the players vote for the next map.

## The rules of the field

- **No flags, no morale, no gold.** The fight is only between the two armies.
- **Fall and fight on.** When your soldier dies, you can take over one of your own formation's soldiers, as in Captain mode. About 3 seconds after you fall, the spectator card on one of your own soldiers says **Press F to take control**. Press F.
- **Fleeing soldiers leave the battle** once they come within 25 m of the map edge, or after fleeing for 2 minutes. On the large campaign maps they would otherwise often never reach the edge, and the round could not end.
- **Leading counts as playing.** The game kicks a player whose soldier hasn't moved or looked around for 3 minutes. Giving your formation orders resets that timer, so a commander leading from the order bar is never kicked for it.
- **Horses** run at 85% of the stock multiplayer top speed.
- **Bows and crossbows** draw and reload at 75% of the stock speed. This holds for players and AI soldiers alike, on foot and mounted.

## Maps

Commander Battle is fought on the campaign's field-battle maps. The loading screen, the map vote and the scoreboard name each one by its look, such as "Wooded Plains with a lake" or "Steppe 7".

- **The armies start where a singleplayer field battle would place them** on the same map: infantry in front, archers behind, cavalry on the flanks, horse archers on the right flank, and the general behind the centre of the line.
- **Every round starts a little differently.** Each army may start up to 40 m to the side, 40 m further back or 15 m further forward, turned a little, and formations of the same kind may swap places. The armies never start outside the map.
- **Map vote.** After each match, the players vote for the next map. They can also call a vote to change the map.

## Known limitations

- **Agent limit.** The game allows about 2,000 soldiers and horses in a battle. Two armies of up to about 620 soldiers each fit, but cavalry-heavy armies, whose horses count too, come closer to the limit.
