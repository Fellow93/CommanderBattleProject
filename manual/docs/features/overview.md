# The Alt Overview

Hold **Left Alt** to see what your side is doing: what the AI has its formations doing, where they are heading, where your fellow players are, and the general's plans.

## The AI status panel

While you hold Left Alt, a panel on the left of the screen shows what your side's AI is doing:

- **the AI's tactic** for the battle;
- **for every formation:** its troops, its behaviour, its order, and how many metres it still has to go.

A formation the general took over shows as **led by the general** (see [The General](general.md#ai-formations-obey)).

### The main formation

The AI's tactic is built around one **main formation**, marked "(main)" in the panel. The AI's archers and cavalry take their places from it. When every infantry formation is led by a player, the main formation can be a player's.

If you lead the main formation, the panel shows a highlighted notice and you get a message, so you know the AI's line is set by where you go. Another message tells you when it stops being yours.

The panel ends with a reminder that **N** opens the formation list, where you change formation and troops. The formation list also shows each AI-led formation's behaviour.

## Destination markers

With Left Alt held, a marker shows where each formation led by the AI or by the general is heading: the position of its move order, or the enemy formation it is charging.

**Suggestion markers** are blue. A commander sees one for the general's latest suggestion to his formation; the general sees one for each suggestion he gave. See [Players get suggestions](general.md#players-get-suggestions).

## The general's drawn orders

While you hold Left Alt, you also see the paths your general drew in general mode: yellow lines on the ground, each with its label at the middle. Only your own side sees them. They are gone when the next round starts. See [Drawn orders](general.md#drawn-orders).

## Player markers

Left Alt puts a **green marker with the name** over each allied player within 100 m of your soldier. While you are dead and spectating, it is 100 m from the camera.

- Enemy players never get one.
- A spectator who is on neither side sees the names of players of both sides.
- A player general has no name marker: his formation's label names him instead.

## Formation labels

Beside the icon of each formation a player leads, a label names its troops and its commander, such as "Khan's Guard (Fellow)".

- **Both sides' player formations** are labelled, yours and the enemy's. AI formations have none.
- **A player general's formation** gets an icon and a label of its own. An AI general has neither.
- **The labels show whenever the icons do:** on Left Alt and while the order bar is open.
- **An enemy formation's label hides with its icon** while that formation is out of sight.
- A label changes when its commander changes troops or formation.
- A spectator who is on neither side sees both sides' labels.
