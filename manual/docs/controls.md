# Controls

Commander Battle adds a few keys to the stock multiplayer controls. The order bar, the cheer wheel and the rest of the stock keys work as in the other multiplayer modes.

## Everyone

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **N** | Opens the formation list: every formation of your side, who leads it, and its troops. Take a free formation or change your troops here. See [Commanding a Formation](features/commanding.md#the-formation-list-n). |
| **Left Alt** (hold) | Shows the AI status panel, the formations' destination markers, the general's drawn orders, the names of allied players within 100 m and the labels of player-led formations. See [The Alt Overview](features/overview.md). |
| **TAB** | The scoreboard, with the formation each player leads. |
| **F** | After you fall: takes control of the soldier the camera follows, when the spectator card says **Press F to take control**. Only your own formation's soldiers. |
| **Esc** | Closes the troop selection screen, then the formation list. Otherwise opens the escape menu, where admins find the **Admin Panel**. |
| Stock cheer wheel | Your formation's soldiers cheer with you. See [Cheer with your men](features/commanding.md#cheer-with-your-men). |

## Commanders

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **F1-F9** | The stock order bar: movement, facing, formation and the other orders. |
| **Number keys** | Pick a formation in the order bar. |
| **Mouse wheel** (order bar open) | Raises or lowers the order camera. |

## The general

The general uses the same order bar, plus these:

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **F1-F9**, **number keys** | Order the AI's formations, one at a time, or send a suggestion to a player's formation. See [The General's orders](features/general.md#the-generals-orders). |
| **Ctrl** + **number key** | Also picks a single formation: the general never has more than one selected. |
| **Ctrl+C** | Enters and leaves general mode, while your soldier is alive and the round is being fought. |
| **Esc** (general mode) | Closes the order bar first, then leaves general mode. |
| **Mouse** (general mode) | Turns the view. Look down to see the field. |
| **Mouse wheel** (general mode) | Zooms the general mode camera in and out. |
| **Left Ctrl** (hold, general mode) | Shows the cursor and holds the view still, so you can use the menu, draw and click labels. |
| **Left Ctrl + left mouse button**, drag (Path tool) | Draws a path along the ground; a label box follows. |
| **Left Ctrl + left click** on a label (Delete tool) | Deletes that path. |

## Server hosts

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Alt+~** (multiplayer lobby) | Opens the game's console, where `customserver.gettoken` gives you a token to list your server. |
