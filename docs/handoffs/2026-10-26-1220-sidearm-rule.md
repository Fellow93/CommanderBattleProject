## What was done

The user asked for a sidearm rule:
- units with a spear or pike carry the sidearm that fits their culture best;
- units with a sword, axe or mace carry none;
- follow-up: neither do swung two-handers (two-handed axes, rhomphaia...).

**The rule, as applied (weapon setups, `AlternativeEquipmentOnSpawn slot="Item2"` without item clears the sidearm):**
- **Keep the sidearm:** spears, pikes, the Empire menavlion (a spear), lances, cavalry spears, and skirmishers (javelins, jereeds, pila: their sidearm is their only melee weapon).
- **No sidearm:**
  - one-handed sword, axe and mace setups, both foot and mounted (Vlandian mace cavalry included);
  - two-handers: Sturgian two-handed axes, Aserai tabar and bardiche, Battanian falx, long falx and rhomphaia, Khuzait glaive (its sabre also left the shield slot), Vlandian bill and voulge.
- **Unchanged:** archers and horse archers keep their sidearm. It is their only melee weapon, and the plan says archers carry one.
- **Culture fit:** the existing sidearms already fit, with one exception. **The Sturgian levy spearman now carries a raider axe (`mp_sturgia_axe`) in place of `mp_sturgia_sword`**: Nordic levies carried axes and swords were for the better-off. The other sidearms:
  - Vlandian short, arming and long swords;
  - the Empire's gladius and spatha, maces for its cavalry;
  - Khuzait sabres, maces for the lancers;
  - Aserai flyssa, kaskara and scimitar;
  - Battanian swords;
  - Sturgian northern swords, and two-handed mauls for the riders.
- Perk descriptions no longer name the dropped sidearm. Each class file's header states the rule.
- `README.md`: a "Sidearms" bullet. The Vlandia setup table was stale (it listed setups from before the variance pass) and now matches the XML.
- `docs/plans/troop-types.md`: the conventions record the user's rule. The old "levies always have a sidearm" line now reads "weapon + shield".
