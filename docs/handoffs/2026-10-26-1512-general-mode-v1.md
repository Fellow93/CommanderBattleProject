## What was done

Built to `docs/plans/general-mode.md`: the user's decisions, the design from the research, and an "As built" section added by the implementing Opus agent.

**General mode** (client, `CommanderBattle.View/CommanderBattleGeneralModeView.cs`, `GeneralModeVM.cs`, prefabs `CommanderBattleGeneralMode.xml` and `CommanderBattleGeneralModeInput.xml`, two brushes in `CommanderBattle.xml`):
- **Entering and leaving:** F1 enters and leaves, for a player general only, while the round is in progress. ESC also leaves.
- **Camera:** `MissionScreen.CustomCamera` starts 150 m up, 60° down. WASD pans at 0.9 × height per second; the wheel zooms between 60 and 400 m; it stays inside the boundary.
- **Agent:** `MissionMainAgentController.IsDisabled`, with movement zeroed every tick, and `SetDisplayDialog(true)`.
- **Leaves automatically** on a hit to him or his horse (`OnAgentHit`, or a health drop as fallback), on death, on losing the general slot, and at round end.
- **Two layers:** order 13 draws and holds the menu; order 12 is a full-screen input layer underneath, active in general mode only, that takes clicks on the ground.
- **Path tool:**
  - drag, with a point every max(3 m, 0.03 × height); presses shorter than 5 m are ignored;
  - the line is thinned to 64 points on release;
  - then the stock `ShowTextInquiry` popup asks for a label (1–40 characters).
- **Delete tool:** labels become buttons; clicking one requests its deletion.
- **Drawing:** paths are screen-space widgets (rotated segments plus a label at the midpoint), projected each mission tick. They show on Left Alt and always in general mode.

**Stock order UI:** `CommanderBattle.View/CommanderBattleOrderUIHandler.cs` subclasses `MissionGauntletMultiplayerOrderUIHandler`, and its `TickInput` returns early for the general (no "no unit under command" message). It is created directly in `CommanderBattleMissionViews.cs`. The csproj gains references to `TaleWorlds.MountAndBlade.GauntletUI` and `.ViewModelCollection`.

**Network:**
- `CommanderBattle/Network/RequestGeneralOrder.cs`, `RequestDeleteGeneralOrder.cs`, `GeneralOrderAdded.cs`, `GeneralOrderRemoved.cs`; data and limits in `CommanderBattle/GeneralOrders.cs`.
- Server: `CommanderBattle/MissionMultiplayerCommanderBattle.GeneralOrders.cs`.
  - **Checks:** only the side's general slot commander, only during a round; points inside the boundaries, at least 2 kept; the label cleaned (an empty one becomes "Path").
  - **Caps:** 10 orders per side, the oldest removed.
  - **Sending:** to the side's peers only. Orders are re-sent on team change and on synchronisation.
  - **Clearing:** at round end (with a removal per order) and silently in `OnClearScene`.
  - **Log:** "<name> (<side> general) drew path <id> '<label>' (<n> points)" and "... deleted path ...".
- Client: `MissionMultiplayerCommanderBattleClient` keeps orders per side, with `OnGeneralOrdersChanged`.

**Docs:** README "General mode" section, two "How it works" rows, 6 checklist items. Plan status in `docs/plans/README.md`; I fixed the general-role row there (6 bodyguards, phase 0 played).
