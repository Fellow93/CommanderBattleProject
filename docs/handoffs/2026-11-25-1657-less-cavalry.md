2026-11-25 16:57

## What was done

- **Unit power prices being mounted.** `tools/UnitEditor/unit-editor-core.js`: the mount's share is now `MountedBonus` 0.25 (ours, not RBM's) plus RBM's share for the horse's hit points and barding. Tooltip in `index.html`, `README.md` of the editor, and the by-hand cavalry test updated.
- **Mounted formation budget 44 → 25.** `CommanderBattleSettings.MountedFormationBudget`, and the editor's `TROOP_BUDGET` copy. Derived: over the six cultures the foot classes average 1.55 power a man and the mounted ones 3.41 (harmonic mean per class, as a levelled class fields), so 54 × 1.55 / 3.41 ≈ 24.5.
- **Empire levelled per class** with the editor's `levelClass` numbers, written by hand into `Module/ModuleData/cb_mpclassdivisions_empire.xml` (9 multipliers, mostly ±0.01; akontistae 0.97 → 0.92, skoutatoi 0.41 → 0.44). Infantry, ranged and cavalry formations come out at about 70 (men × power); the two horse archer units at about 100, which levelling inside a class can't change.
- **The other five cultures levelled** the same way (after the first commit): 37 multipliers in `cb_mpclassdivisions_{vlandia,khuzait,aserai,sturgia,battania}.xml`; heavy classes field fewer men, light ones more. The README's Vlandia table follows. Classes are level inside themselves, not against each other: e.g. Vlandia infantry 93, ranged 87, cavalry 104; Battania cavalry 72 against ranged 95.
